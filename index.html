Hadley Wood Shul · Barnet, North London

Welcome to Hadley Wood Jewish Community

A warm, modern and friendly synagogue in Hadley Wood, on the edge of Barnet. We are a small United Synagogue community with a purpose-built shul, a thriving Cheder and a famously good Kiddush. Whoever you are, and wherever you are on your Jewish journey, you are welcome here.

About our shul

North London’s best-kept secret

Hadley Wood Jewish Community, known locally as Hadley Wood Shul, is an intimate Orthodox synagogue close to Barnet and Cockfosters. We became an independent member of the United Synagogue in 2012, and in 2018 we completed a full rebuild of our home on Lancaster Avenue, giving us an outstanding, purpose-built, modern synagogue building.

What makes us special is the people. With over 250 members we are big enough to have a full programme of services and activities, and small enough that you will be greeted by name. Our community is led by Rabbi Toby and Rebbetzen Bracha Weiniger, who joined us in January 2026.

Life at Hadley Wood Synagogue is busy: Shabbat and festival services, film evenings, days out, visiting speakers, shiurim, keep-fit classes, a poetry club, and our much-loved Cheder for children in school years 1 to 6.

Fast into town

Hadley Wood station runs direct trains to Kings Cross and Moorgate. The Piccadilly line is at Cockfosters and the Northern line at High Barnet.

Green on the doorstep

Trent Country Park, once a hunting ground of Henry VIII, is just up the road with woods, gorges and protected wildlife.

Jewish life nearby

Wolfson Hillel primary school (rated Outstanding), a kosher butcher and mikveh facilities are within ten minutes.

Kosher shopping

Borehamwood and Edgware are about fifteen minutes away, with Hendon around twenty.

Living in Hadley Wood

The shul you’ve never heard of that you’ll never want to leave

Hadley Wood is one of North London’s most sought-after addresses, set in beautiful countryside yet only eleven miles from the centre of London. It is a wonderful place to raise a family, with space to grow, excellent schools and quick transport links.

Heathrow, Luton and Stansted airports are all around forty minutes away by car, traffic permitting.

Our story

From a living room to a purpose-built synagogue

Hadley Wood Jewish Community began in the late 1980s, meeting on Friday nights in the home of the late Tony Brooks and his wife Bonnie. Services moved to the home of Cheryl and Steve Pack, whose lounge became a shul every Shabbat for seven years, complete with a grand piano and a great Kiddush.

The community grew, held its first High Holy Day services in 1998, and in 2002 bought a house on Lancaster Avenue with the support of local residents, interest-free loans and the United Synagogue. Refurbished in 2009 and fully rebuilt in 2018, both in loving memory of the late Marsha Rogers, our building today is as inviting as ever.

  1. 1989 First Friday night services held in members’ homes in Hadley Wood.
  2. 1998 First High Holy Day services and first Bar Mitzvah; over 300 people now join us for the Yomim Noraim.
  3. 2002 A permanent home purchased at 8 Lancaster Avenue.
  4. 2012 Hadley Wood Jewish Community becomes an independent member of the United Synagogue.
  5. 2018 Full rebuild completed: a modern, purpose-built shul.
  6. 2026 Rabbi Toby and Rebbetzen Bracha Weiniger join as our rabbinic couple.
