Cheder · Hebrew school

Hadley Wood Jewish Community Cheder

Warmth, creativity and learning. Our Cheder gives children a solid grounding in Jewish knowledge, fluent and confident Hebrew reading, a strong sense of Jewish identity, and lots of fun along the way.

The essentials

Small classes, big hearts

The Hadley Wood Jewish Community Cheder has always prided itself on its warm, creative and fun environment. Led by our Rabbi and Rebbetzen, Toby and Bracha Weiniger, it is a thriving centre for Jewish education with small classes and a stimulating curriculum.

Every Jewish festival is celebrated in full swing, and innovative lesson design means children learn through discussion, stories, arts and crafts, and questions. Cheder is open to members and non-members alike.

What children learn

A curriculum that grows with your child

Hebrew reading

Our Hebrew reading coordinator, Mrs Diana Lazarus, leads a team of community members who work alongside the Cheder staff so every child reads fluently and confidently.

Jewish knowledge & festivals

A dynamic curriculum covering the Jewish year, with every festival celebrated properly, plus the weekly Parsha told as a story children can question and challenge.

Years 5 & 6 with the Rabbi and Rebbetzen

Older children are taught directly by our rabbinic couple, exploring Chumash, Halacha and Gemara through discussion as they prepare for Bar and Bat Mitzvah.

Identity & friendship

Children leave our Cheder with a strong sense of who they are, and with friends made in a warm, creative and fun environment.
