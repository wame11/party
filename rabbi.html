Our rabbinic couple

Rabbi Toby & Rebbetzen Bracha Weiniger

Rabbi Toby and Rebbetzen Bracha Weiniger became the rabbinic couple of Hadley Wood Jewish Community in January 2026, bringing warmth, energy and a passion for education to Hadley Wood Shul.

Rabbi

Rabbi Toby Weiniger

Rabbi Toby Weiniger joined Hadley Wood Jewish Community as its Rabbi in January 2026. Alongside his role at the shul he serves as Halachic Advisor to Hatzola HBS, the volunteer emergency ambulance service for Hadley Wood, Barnet and Southgate.

Before coming to Hadley Wood, Rabbi Weiniger and Rebbetzen Bracha served on the rabbinic team at St John’s Wood Synagogue from 2021 to 2025, where they took particular responsibility for children and youth, young families and community education.

Rabbi Weiniger studied for several years at Yeshivat Har Etzion in Israel and received his rabbinic ordination (semicha) from Rabbi S. F. Zimmerman, Rov and Av Beit Din of the Federation in London. Since 2022 he has been a regular lecturer at the Jewish Learning Exchange (JLE) in Golders Green, teaching Gemara and Halacha to young adults, and he is a sought-after speaker in schools, universities, shuls and workplaces.

He also graduated with a first-class law degree from the London School of Economics (LSE) in 2022, holds postgraduate qualifications in law, and is completing his professional qualification as a lawyer alongside his rabbinic work.

Rebbetzen

Rebbetzen Bracha Weiniger

Rebbetzen Bracha Weiniger joined Hadley Wood Jewish Community as its Rebbetzen in January 2026. Together with Rabbi Toby she previously served on the rabbinic team at St John’s Wood Synagogue, leading work with children, youth and young families and running community education.

After her A-Levels, Rebbetzen Bracha studied at Michlelet Mevaseret Yerushalayim (MMY) Seminary in Jerusalem. She is a certified Kallah teacher and a passionate Bat Mitzvah teacher, and leads our Cheder alongside Rabbi Toby.

She holds a first-class MSci in Chemistry with Medicinal Chemistry from Imperial College London and, alongside her communal role, is pursuing a PhD in Chemical Biology as part of Imperial’s Centre for Doctoral Training programme.
