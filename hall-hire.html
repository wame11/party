Hall hire · Hadley Wood, Barnet

A beautiful, modern space for your celebration

Our purpose-built hall at Hadley Wood Synagogue, with its garden marquee and fully fitted kosher kitchen, is available to hire for simchas, kiddushim, family celebrations, community gatherings and meetings.

Enquire about hall hire 020 8143 2580
Why hire here

Everything in one place

Rebuilt in 2018, Hadley Wood Jewish Community’s building was designed for celebrating. The hall opens onto the garden, so a Shabbat Kiddush, an engagement party or a milestone birthday can flow from inside to out. The kitchen is set up for kosher catering, and the whole building is easy to reach from Barnet, Cockfosters, Enfield and across North London, with Hadley Wood station a short walk away.

We keep things simple: tell us what you are planning and our office will talk you through availability, what is included and the hire charge.

How it works

Booking in three easy steps

  1. Tell us about your event. Email or call the shul office with your preferred date, the type of event and roughly how many guests.
  2. We confirm availability and cost. The office will explain what is included and the hire charge for your event.
  3. Plan the details. Arrange your caterer, layout and timings with us, then enjoy the day.

Bar and Bat Mitzvahs are arranged separately. If you are planning a Bar or Bat Mitzvah at Hadley Wood Shul, please contact Rabbi Toby directly rather than the hall hire team. Find out more about Bar and Bat Mitzvah.
