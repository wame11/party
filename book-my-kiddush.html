Key information

How do I book a Kiddush?

Choose an available date on the calendar, select your preferred sponsorship package, and complete the booking form.

What about Bar and Bat Mitzvahs?

By all means book your Kiddush, but please be aware there is a separate process for booking Bar and Bat Mitzvah celebrations through the office.

Can I cancel or change my booking?

Yes. Please contact the office at office@hwjc.org.uk for help with cancellations or changes.

Who can see the amount I’ve sponsored?

Only the Shul office.

What if a date says “Booked”?

That means it’s already taken. Please select another available Shabbat or festival date.

Questions? Email office@hwjc.org.uk.
