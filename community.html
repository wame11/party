What is a Kiddush?

Kiddush is the blessing said over wine to sanctify Shabbat. At shul, “the Kiddush” has come to mean the gathering after the Shabbat morning service, when everyone shares food and drink, catches up and celebrates together. At Hadley Wood it has been our signature dish since the very first services.

Sponsor the Shabbat Kiddush

As a small community we rely on a Kiddush sponsor every week. Sponsoring is a lovely way to celebrate a simcha, mark a birthday or anniversary, remember a loved one on a yahrzeit, or simply support your community.

Read my Haftorah

Take part in the service

The Haftorah is the reading from the Prophets that follows the Torah reading on Shabbat morning. Reading it is a wonderful way to mark a Bar Mitzvah anniversary, a yahrzeit or a special occasion, or simply to play your part in the service.

Use Read my Haftorah to see which Shabbatot are available and book the date you would like to read. If you have not read before or would like some help preparing, Rabbi Toby will be delighted to guide you.

Bar & Bat Mitzvah

Celebrating your child’s Bar or Bat Mitzvah at Hadley Wood Shul

A Bar Mitzvah (for boys at 13) or Bat Mitzvah (for girls at 12) marks the moment a young person takes on the responsibilities of Jewish adulthood. It is one of the happiest occasions in the life of our community, and we celebrate every one.

Whether you are hoping for a full leining of the Torah portion, a Haftorah, a d’var Torah or a Bat Mitzvah presentation, Rabbi Toby and Rebbetzen Bracha, an experienced Bat Mitzvah teacher, will work with your family to plan a meaningful day and support your child in the run-up.

To start the conversation, please contact Rabbi Toby directly.

Hatzola HBS

Hatzola Hadley Wood, Barnet & Southgate

Hatzola HBS is a volunteer emergency ambulance service providing rapid, professional medical care across Hadley Wood, Barnet, Southgate, Cockfosters, Whetstone and Totteridge. Our Rabbi, Toby Weiniger, serves as its Halachic Advisor.

Operated entirely by highly trained volunteers, Hatzola HBS delivers immediate pre-hospital emergency treatment and, where needed, safe transport to hospital. Because responders live and work locally, they can often be at the scene within minutes. The service works alongside the NHS and other emergency agencies, and is available to everyone, regardless of faith, background or circumstance.

Hatzola HBS is a registered charity and depends entirely on supporters to maintain its ambulances, equip its responders and keep delivering life-saving care.

In a life-threatening emergency always call 999. The Hatzola HBS emergency line is published on the Hatzola HBS website; save it to your phone before you need it.
